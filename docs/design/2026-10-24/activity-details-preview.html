A small first step

Option 2 groups repeated thinking notices into timed phases, alongside the existing tools. Expand a phase for timing and source; open Turn details for usage and context events. It leaves assistant text alone.

Option 3 also collects progress updates. Codex can use its explicit commentary label. Claude, Copilot and ordinary MTPLX text need a conservative rule or a manual “Move to Activity” action, because a short sentence can still be an important answer.

This is a reviewable prototype, not an enabled behavior change. Provider availability is based on the installed adapters/SDK, not a promise that every model emits every event. Raw internal reasoning is not reproduced in this study.
